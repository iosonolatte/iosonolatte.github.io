About

I spent the first decade-plus of my career building software that people actually depend on — banking apps at UOB, trading platform at Brightoil, customer platforms at Equinix. That work taught me the part that matters most: shipping, and keeping it running.

Today, at Capgemini Singapore, I build and extend client web platforms with React / Next.js / TypeScript on AWS — frontend, Node.js services and CI/CD included. Outside client work I ship AI-native products on my own: LLM integration, RAG, local model inference with Ollama, and engine bridging (KataGo). I like problems where the answer isn't in the docs yet, and I'm comfortable sitting with a customer in their environment until it works.
